Our Story

Umbilo Car Wash & Shisanyama started as a humble side hustle operating out of a small lot with a single hose and an open braai drum. Recognizing that motorists were tired of waiting on dusty curbs while their vehicles were cleaned, we introduced fresh braai platters, cold drinks, and local music. Today, we are Umbilo's premier lifestyle hub where automotive care meets true South African hospitality!
